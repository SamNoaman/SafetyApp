SOS Family Alert

Privacy Policy

Introduction

SOS Family Alert ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal safety application designed to help you send emergency and non-emergency alerts to your chosen contacts. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your data is used.

Information We Collect

SOS Family Alert collects and uses the following data locally on your device:

  • Contact Information: Names and phone numbers of contacts you manually add to the app. This data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Location Data: Your GPS coordinates are accessed in real time when you use the Emergency or Non-Emergency features to share your location with your contacts via SMS.
  • Camera and Microphone: The app accesses your camera and microphone to record video during an emergency alert. Video is saved to your Photos library and attached to SMS messages you send.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send SMS messages containing your location to contacts you select.
  • To record and attach emergency video evidence to SMS messages.
  • To display your real-time location on a map during emergency recording.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers, databases, or cloud storage. Your contacts, location data, and videos are never uploaded to or processed by any external service.

Third-Party Services

SOS Family Alert does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external services used are:

  • Apple Maps: Location links are generated using Apple Maps URLs so your contacts can view your location.
  • iOS Messages: SMS messages are sent through the built-in iOS messaging framework. Message delivery is handled by your carrier.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions, all of which are optional and can be managed in iOS Settings:

  • Location (When In Use): Required to share your location in emergency and non-emergency alerts.
  • Camera: Required to record video during emergency alerts.
  • Microphone: Required to capture audio during emergency video recording.
  • Photo Library (Add Only): Used to save emergency video recordings to your Photos.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. The only data shared is what you explicitly send via SMS to your chosen contacts.

Children's Privacy

SOS Family Alert does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is intended for general audiences concerned about personal safety.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
